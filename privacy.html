Privacy policy

Personal Record Backup — effective October 2, 2026.

Data and access

The owner selects files for backup and grants access through Google's OAuth consent screen. The utility processes selected file contents, paths, sizes, timestamps, and checksums to upload and verify copies. The drive.file permission limits access to Drive files created or explicitly opened with this app.

Storage and use

Backup records are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Operational metadata and OAuth credentials stay on the owner's device. Authentication files are excluded from backup. No backup records or credentials are published on this website.

The owner uses the records for personal reference, restoration, and analysis. The utility does not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers, or send it to model-training services.

Retention and control

The owner controls retention of long-term records. QA execution results have a maximum retention period of 30 days. The owner can delete backup files in Google Drive and revoke access in Google Account settings. Revoking access does not itself delete files already stored.

Other services and contact

Google Drive and GitHub hosting are subject to their respective providers' policies. Questions can be directed to the support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
